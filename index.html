    About Me

    I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), and a member of the HKUST NLP Group led by Prof. Junxian He, who is also my PhD supervisor. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I was previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

    I am currently a Research Intern at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present).
